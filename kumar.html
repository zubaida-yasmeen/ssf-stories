Walking Together, Giving Back Together

10,000 steps a day. 30 days. A little friendly competition. And an alumni community that found a new way to stay connected and give back.

Giving back doesn’t always mean giving money or time. Sometimes, it means inspiring someone else to take the next step.

SSF alumnus Gowriyokeshkumar S knows the value of community support firsthand. After completing his B.E. in Electrical and Electronics Engineering with the support of an SSF scholarship, he went on to build a career as an Electrical Engineer in the R&D department at Lucas TVS.

Wanting to give something back to the SSF community, Gowriyokeshkumar and a friend came up with a simple idea: a 30-day walking challenge for SSF alumni.

Using the Pacer app, they encouraged alumni to aim for at least 10,000 steps a day. A leaderboard added a little friendly competition and soon, alumni were motivating one another to keep going.

An average of 15 alumni actively tracked their steps throughout the month. One participant went the extra mile, clocking an incredible 599,561 steps in 30 days.

But the real impact went beyond the numbers.

The challenge gave alumni a reason to reconnect, encourage one another and stay part of a community that had once supported them.

Sometimes, paying it forward starts with something as simple as taking a walk together.

And the next challenge is already on its way!
