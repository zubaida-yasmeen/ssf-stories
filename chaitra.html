From a Dream to Tokyo

Chaitra dreamed of living and working abroad. She never imagined her very first job would take her all the way to Tokyo.

Chaitra Saravana had always dreamed of living and working outside India.

“I wanted to move to a country that was culturally rich, like India, and experience it for myself,” says the SSF alumna.

A graduate of BNM Institute of Technology, Chaitra completed her degree in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning with the support of an SSF scholarship.

Her hard work soon opened an unexpected door. Through campus recruitment, she landed her first job and discovered that her new workplace would be in Japan.

“I was so happy that my first job location was Japan,” she recalls.

Her mother needed some convincing, but what began with hesitation soon turned into pride.

Today, Chaitra lives in Tokyo and works as a developer at Jems Co. Ltd. She has made new friends, navigated a new culture and grown increasingly comfortable in a city that was once only part of a dream.

“We had a basic Japanese language course before we moved. Today, I speak much better Japanese and know my way around Tokyo,” she says.

Of course, there are things she still misses, her friends, Bangalore’s masala dosa and her favourite biryani spots. But she is embracing the new life she once imagined for herself.

Her message to other students is simple:

“Dream big, but dreams alone are not enough. You have to put in the effort at every step.”

For Chaitra, the dream was independence and the opportunity to work in a foreign country. With the right support and her own determination, she turned that dream into reality.
